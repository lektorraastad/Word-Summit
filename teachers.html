Word Summit for teachers

A free reading site for English students in 8th–10th grade. Students read texts at their level, answer five questions, and climb a level ladder from A1 to C2.

    Start a class

    1. Give students the address www.wordsummit.no and ask them to bookmark it.
    2. The first visit is a reading test of about 10 minutes. Students can stop and continue later.
    3. After that, students choose texts themselves. 10–15 minutes at the end of a lesson works well.
    4. Progress is saved in the student's own browser. Students must always use the same browser on the same PC. A backup file (the Aa menu → “Save backup file”) can move it to another browser.

    See your class's results

    1. Students open “My report” and copy their report code.
    2. Collect the codes in one place. The easiest way is a form with one question, “Paste your report code”, one form per class. A text assignment in your learning platform also works.
    3. Copy all the answers and paste them into Class Reports below.on the teacher page, www.wordsummit.no/teachers.

    Class Reports

    The codes are read on this PC only. Nothing is uploaded or saved, so the table is empty again when you close the page.

    What the levels mean

    The 11 levels are named after the CEFR scale (A1 to C2), with a “+” step between each. Students see the same descriptions on the site.

      Students move up after passing 3 texts at a level (2 at a “+” level). A pass is 4 of 5 points. Two weak texts in a row, or four texts in a row without a pass, move a student down one step.

      Good to know

      Who made it

      Word Summit is made by an English teacher at a Norwegian lower secondary school. It is free and has no ads.

      How the texts are made

      The texts and questions are written with AI, to fixed rules for each level. Every text has passed automatic checks, and a second AI reader has checked it for errors, wrong facts and unclear questions. Not every text has been read by a teacher, so mistakes can exist.

      Levels

      The levels are estimates, made when the texts were written, and are for guidance only. They are not grades and should not be used for grading. The student report says the same: “This report shows your reading. It is not a grade.”

      Privacy

      There is no login, no accounts and no tracking. Progress is saved only in each student's browser. The report code holds the first name the student typed (optional), levels and scores. It is decoded only on the teacher's own PC, and nothing pasted into Class Reports is uploaded.
